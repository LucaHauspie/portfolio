## Intro

What I’m building right now: my first steps into 3D, in Blender and in the browser.

## TWOJEYS

My first 3D project: a conceptual header for TWOJEYS to announce a new ring. The ring sits in a workshop garage I’m building in Blender, between the tools, cables and tyres it was made with.

Right now I’m working on the rigging and animation. Texturing and lighting come last, once everything moves the way it should.

## Sunday Session

A skateboarding experience in the browser, built with Three.js and WebGPU, which I’m still learning. Push, carve and pop tricks across a small skatepark; every kickflip gets a name and a style rating, from sketchy to clean.

Next up: more shaders to give the park its look, and optimising the shapes so it runs smoothly.
