## Intro

What I’m building right now, and what’s next: my bachelor thesis AERATA, my first steps into 3D, in Blender and in the browser and an upcoming five weeks of live visuals in January,

## AERATA

My bachelor thesis, in the making with Margot Lievens and Leon Nolf: an immersive, abstract installation in one small room in the Pompenzaal of Transfo Zwevegem, for Transfo Intiem 2026. The theme is breath.

 Coal, steam, generators and canal water once kept Transfo alive, but only while people worked it. In AERATA visitors take over the workers’ shift: through one large touchscreen and a physical lever they wake four machines, while old TVs, fog machines, LED lines and projection on piles of industrial junk will answer all around them.

## Live visuals

In January 2027 I’m spending five weeks on one thing: live visuals. Visuals that react to the music and that I play live, like a VJ, built in Resolume and TouchDesigner.

This is where I want to end up: making visuals for artists, clubs and festivals. Five weeks to go deep, test it in front of music and come out with a first live set.

## TWOJEYS

My first 3D project: a conceptual header for TWOJEYS to announce a new ring. The ring sits in a workshop garage I’m building in Blender, between the tools, cables and tyres it was made with.

Right now I’m working on the rigging and animation. Texturing and lighting come last, once everything moves the way it should.

## Sunday Session

A skateboarding experience in the browser, built with Three.js and WebGPU, which I’m still learning. Push, carve and pop tricks across a small skatepark; every kickflip gets a name and a style rating, from sketchy to clean.

Next up: more shaders to give the park its look, and optimising the shapes so it runs smoothly.
