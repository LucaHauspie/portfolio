## Intro

The brief: design the website for the first conference of TYPE01, a half-yearly magazine about graphic design and typography.

## Body

Two days in London on typography in print, motion design and branding, for experienced and upcoming type designers. I gave the site a louder, more typographic take on TYPE01’s house style.

## Step 1: First tryout

My very first design got the message wrong. Type drawn into London’s skyline looked like a website about typography in London, while it had to feel like the conference of the newest type magazine.

## Step 2: Research

So I went back to research: editorial magazine spreads and posters with experimental, distorted type. That showed me where the magazine side of TYPE01 should come from.

## Step 3: A new direction

The research turned into a new styleframe: acid green on black, with the TYPE01 letters broken up and floating like a magazine cover.

## Step 4: Wireframe & UI research

Then I built a wireframe, and it didn’t work: I hadn’t looked at how other event sites handle a schedule, filters or a mobile menu. I collected UI references to fix that, from festival timetables to filter pop-ups on mobile.

## Step 5: Second tryout

My second tryout took the green direction across the whole site. I liked it a lot, but with only green and black everything had the same weight, so I needed a third colour.

## Step 6: Final

Adding purple gave the site the contrast it missed. The final design works on two breakpoints, desktop and phone, each with its own prototype flow in Figma and Auto Layout keeping it fluid in between.
