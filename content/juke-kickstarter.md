## Intro

The brief: make the video for a Kickstarter campaign of your choice. I picked Juke, handmade snowboards by two friends from Massachusetts.

## Body

Being free to choose meant I could go for a vibe I love, skate and snowboard culture. The style comes from old cartoons like Samurai Jack: flat shapes, hard angles, big type.

## Step 1: The brand

Juke started in 2010 as a passion project of two friends. After years of building, testing and riding, this Kickstarter puts their handmade boards on sale for the first time.

## Step 2: Style

Samurai Jack and Saul Bass posters set the look: flat colour, sharp cut-out shapes, chunky hand-cut type. The styleframe locked it in, with “JUKE” reflected in the goggles.

## Step 3: Script & storyboard

Seven scenes in 30 seconds. A slow skier gets overtaken, JUKE sweeps across the goggles, and the story moves through made by hand, made with love and made locally before it ends on a call to donate.


## Biggest challenge

Presenting Juke’s real board designs and making them fit in a cartoony video without breaking the style.

## What I've learned

Working fluently with after effects, creating smooth transitions.
